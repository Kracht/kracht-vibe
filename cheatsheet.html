KRACHT 0.5.2 · hard techno drums

Synthesised, not sampled: a kick designer, the techno rumble under it, hats, clap, a 16-step sequencer, and the kick ducking whatever you plug into IN 1+2. PLAY starts the default pattern with the EXHALE kick.

K1–K6 = pages · encoders 1–4 = the four values

KICKTUNE (the body's note, Hz) · FROM (sweep start) · SWEEP (ms down to TUNE) · DECAY
PUNCHCLICK · DRIVE (dB, 4× oversampled) · SHAPE (Soft / Tube / Fold / Hard) · TAIL (the sub under the body)
DRUMSHAT DECAY · HAT TONE · OPEN DECAY · CLAP DECAY
SIDEINPUT (IN 1+2 into the mix) · SIDECHAIN (how deep the kick ducks it) · GLUE (master saturation) · VOLUME
RUMBLERUMBLE (level) · R TONE (low-pass 70–290 Hz) · R DRIVE · DUCK (how hard the kick pushes the rumble away)
SEQTEMPO (120–170) · SWING · REPEAT (note repeat rate) · encoder 4 = LEVEL of the selected lane · SHIFT: QUANTIZE · STRENGTH · LENGTH of the lane (1–16, polymeter)
MIDIK6 again: MAP (KRACHT · GM · MPC · LEARN) · IN CH · OUT CH · OUT (off / notes / clock / both) · DATA pressed: learn the lane's note · in: PC 0–6 preset · CC 20–23 mute · CC 24–27 pattern
TRACKSK6 a third time, the steps without pads: A step · B the lane's value · encoders 1–4 = KICK · HAT · OPEN · CLAP on that step · DATA pressed = on / off

F keys

F1–F4Lane: KICK · HAT · OPEN · CLAP – the pads edit this lane
SHIFT + F1–F4Mute / unmute the lane – on the next bar (struck through; blinking while it waits)
F5PATTERN: the next pattern of the lane's book (four, rolling, gallop, offbeat, push …), switched on the next bar – the key shows > name while it waits. SHIFT + F5: the previous one. Press it several times to skip ahead. Held 1 s: the lane empty
F6PRESET: next kick – EXHALE → KRACHT → PSY → HOUSE → RUMBLE → ZAP → INDUSTRIAL (stopped: you hear it)
DATAKick preset, back and forth (also − +) · held 0.6 s: FOCUS

Pads · keys · transport

PADSThe 16 steps of the lane. Tap again and again: hit → accent → ghost → off. Stopped, a tap plays it
KEYSPlay live: C kick · D E clap · F# G# hat · A# open (every octave). MIDI in by the map on the MIDI page
SHIFT + RETURNK1–K6 play drums – KICK · HAT · OPEN · CLAP · kick accent · ghost hat; again: the pages
PLAYSequencer on / off · STOP stops. In a DAW it follows the host's transport and tempo
A · BEncoder A = VOLUME · encoder B = DUCK RELEASE (in beats: how long the rumble and the input stay down)
HOMEWhat every control does right now · held 1 s: the firmware library
SAVEDBy itself: every value, the preset, steps with overdubs, mutes, the six scenes, MIDI settings – 1.5 s after a change and with the DAW project. Next start: where you left off

Perform

SHIFT + PADS16 FX while held (2× tap: latched) – rows: REPEAT 1 · ½ · ¼ · ⅛ beat · PITCH tape stop · down · up · reverse · FILTER LP · HP · telephone · wash · THROW delay · hall · crush · no kick. Drums only, IN 1+2 keeps pumping
SHIFT + A BFX without pads: A picks, B = amount, SHIFT + + held plays it (twice: latched)
−Note repeat, while K1–K6 are drums (controller: LEFT): hold keys, they repeat at SEQ · REPEAT (tap = on, hold = momentary)
RECOverdub: keys and MIDI into the steps · SHIFT + REC lane back to its pattern
SHIFT + KEYKick tuned to that note
SHIFT + −Fill (K1–K6 as drums: just +; controller: RIGHT): tap = the next half bar into a bar · hold = while held
SHIFT + K1–K6Scenes: recall on the next bar · empty slot or held 1 s: store (steps, mutes, lengths, preset, sound)

The rumble

Every new kick is convolved (FFT) with a dark 2.6 s hall, then saturated, low-passed twice and ducked by the next kick – the rolling sub of hard techno, made from your kick. Change the kick, the rumble follows a moment later.
