# The demo takes, by hand (KRACHT 0.3)

Every take was played on the firmware with the moves below: no DAW, no automation, no edits afterwards. Rendered with
the SDK's wasmtest, one press every 120 ms, pad taps every 110 ms. Mutes and patterns from the book are pressed during
the bar before and land on the one.

Keys: **F1–F4** lane · **SHIFT + F1–F4** mute · **F5 / SHIFT + F5** next / previous pattern · **F6** preset ·
**K1–K6** page · encoders 1–4 on the page shown · keys **C** kick, **D/E** clap.

## 03 · Berghain rumble · RUMBLE · 136 BPM · a pad on IN 1

- Before PLAY: F6 ×4 (RUMBLE). SIDE: input 60, sidechain 92. RUMBLE: rumble 92, tone 12, duck 92. B (release) 0.7.
  DRUMS: hat decay 60, hat tone 30, clap decay 420. Hat, open hat and clap on "out" (F5). Page RUMBLE.
- Bar 2: hat → offbeat + ghost (F2, F5)
- Bar 3: open hat → every other · turn R tone 12 → 55 over three bars
- Bar 4: clap → four only
- Bar 5: hat → rolling
- Bar 6: open hat → offbeat · K3 (DRUMS), hat tone 30 → 60

## 04 · Groove, swung · KRACHT · 138 BPM

- Before PLAY: F6 ×1 (KRACHT). Swing 28, kick decay 200, tune 52, click 50, glue 40, rumble 35.
  Hat → rolling, open hat → offbeat + 16th. **Clap built on the pads:** `..o.X..o..o.X.o.` (ghosts around the
  backbeat – it is not in the book). Open hat and clap muted. Page DRUMS.
- Bar 2: unmute open hat
- Bar 3: unmute clap · hat → gallop
- Bar 4: three taps on kick step 8 (a ghost before the third beat), heard as you tap · hat tone up over two bars
- Bar 5: hat → drive · mute clap
- Bar 6: hat → rolling (SHIFT + F5) · unmute clap

## 05 · Driving, stabs on IN 1 · KRACHT · 145 BPM

- Before PLAY: F6 ×1. Tune 48, decay 320, drive 14 dB, rumble 60, input 55, sidechain 85, release 0.45.
  Hat → sixteenths, open hat → offbeat accent, open hat and clap muted. Page DRUMS.
- From bar 1: one hand opens the hat tone 20 → 95 over the whole take
- Bar 2: unmute open hat
- Bar 3: unmute clap
- Bar 4: kick → double end
- Bar 5: hat → drive, open hat → ghost run
- Bar 6: kick → four, clap → double end

## 08 · Rolling psy, acid on IN 1 · PSY · 148 BPM

- Before PLAY: F6 ×2 (PSY). Rumble 85, release 0.25, duck 95, input 50, sidechain 95, hat level 50.
  Open hat → ghost run, open hat and clap muted.
- Bar 2: unmute open hat
- Bar 3: unmute clap
- Bar 4: hat → rolling
- Bar 5: three taps on kick step 16 (a ghost before the one)
- Bar 6: clap → double end

## 10 · Break and drop · EXHALE · 150 BPM

- Before PLAY: rumble 60, hat → sixteenths. Page RUMBLE.
- Bar 2: kick → four, pickup
- Bar 3: kick → half, mute open hat · turn rumble 60 → 95 over two bars
- Bar 4: mute kick, hat, clap · kick → four (lands silently) · a clap roll on the D key, three beats, getting faster
- Bar 5: unmute all four · rumble back to 65
- Bar 6: kick → rolling
- Bar 7: kick → double end, clap → double end

The synth parts on IN 1 (pad, stabs, acid) are not KRACHT: they stand for whatever you plug in.

## 11 · FX throws · KRACHT · 150 BPM (0.5.0)

SHIFT + pad, each held as written and let go with SHIFT; the pad's velocity 0.8 (tape stop 0.2, soft).

- Before PLAY: F6 ×1 (KRACHT). The default pattern, nothing else changed.
- Bar 4, beat 4: SHIFT + pad 13 (DELAY) for one beat – the throw rings on into bar 5
- Bar 6, beat 4: SHIFT + pad 8 (REVERSE) for one beat
- Bar 8, beats 3–4: SHIFT + pad 3 (REPEAT 1/4)
- Bar 10, beat 1: SHIFT + pad 5 (TAPE STOP), held – the take ends on the halt
